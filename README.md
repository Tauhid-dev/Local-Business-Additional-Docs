Design Principles

* AI should automate repetitive work while keeping humans in control of important decisions.
* Build modular components that can be reused across different industries.
* Prioritise explainability and maintainability over unnecessary complexity.
* Keep the platform scalable for thousands of businesses.
* Continuously evolve the product using customer feedback and measurable outcomes.

⸻

Long-Term Vision

The goal of AI-Magnet Local Business Expert is to become an AI Business Operating System for local service businesses.

Instead of being only a chatbot, the platform will assist businesses throughout the complete customer lifecycle:

Customer Enquiry → AI Conversation → Lead Qualification → Booking → Quotation → Job Management → Invoicing → Payment → Reviews → Customer Retention.

By combining Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), workflow automation, business integrations, and AI-assisted decision support, the platform aims to reduce administrative effort, improve customer experience, and help local businesses grow more efficiently.
